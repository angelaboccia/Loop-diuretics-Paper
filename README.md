# ** Adverse Events to Loop Diuretics combined with guideline-directed medical therapies for Heart Failure: a pharmacovigilance analysis**
#  
## 📌 Background
American guidelines recommend the use of diuretics in combination with GDMT (including angiotensin-converting enzyme [ACE] inhibitors, angiotensin II receptor blockers [ARBs], and angiotensin receptor–neprilysin inhibitors [ARNIs], sodium–glucose co-transporter 2 [SGLT2 inhibitors, mineralocorticoid receptor antagonists [MRAs]) although these agents are not considered disease-modifying therapies.
Despite their established role in symptom relief, loop diuretics are associated with clinically significant adverse effects, including renal dysfunction and electrolyte disturbances such as hyponatremia, hypokalemia, and metabolic alkalosis.

## 🎯 Objective

This study aims to characterize the safety profile of loop diuretics in patients with heart failure by evaluating the reporting of cardiovascular and renal adverse events, specifically cardiac arrhythmias, renal failure, and syncope, using disproportionality analysis.
